Connectcha

A private photo and video app for friends and family. You can only be found by people who have your number.

Get it on your iPhone

Connectcha is in testing, so it comes through Apple's free TestFlight app instead of the App Store. It takes about two minutes.

  1. Install TestFlight. Tap the button below. The App Store opens; tap Get. If you already have TestFlight, skip this.
  2. Come back to this page and tap Join Connectcha.
  3. In TestFlight, tap Accept, then Install.
  4. Open Connectcha, type your phone number and the code we text you, and add your name.
  5. Allow contacts access so the app can find the people you know. The names in your phone stay on your phone.
