Research note compiled 2026-11-16. Note on sourcing: in this session WebFetch to sec.gov, company IR sites, Yahoo Finance, Benzinga, Investing.com, ANS and other hosts was blocked by network policy, so figures below come from search-result extracts of those primary documents and from secondary coverage (trade press, transcript summaries). Where a figure comes only from an aggregator or where sources conflict, that is flagged. Dates are given explicitly; anything before 2026 is labelled.

## Key Question 1: Pure-play SMR / advanced reactor stocks — design, licensing, customers, cash, dilution, stock performance

### Takeaway
2026 has been a year of real regulatory and construction milestones for the listed pure plays (TerraPower and TVA construction permits, X-energy/Dow Long Mott permit, Oklo's DOE safety approval and steel going up at INL, NRC acceptance of NANO's KRONOS application, the first-ever X-energy and Holtec IPOs) combined with a brutal de-rating: NuScale is down roughly 61% YTD (~$4.82 on Nov 12), Oklo roughly 62% YTD (~$23.92 on Nov 13), NANO roughly 48% YTD (~$11.11 on Nov 13), X-energy trades ~52% below its $23 April IPO price and Fermi is down ~68% YTD. All of the pure plays are pre-revenue or near pre-revenue, but balance sheets are unusually strong after heavy at-the-market (ATM) issuance: Oklo $3.13B, X-energy $1.8B, NuScale $1.9B, NANO ~$580M, Terrestrial $276M, Lightbridge $237M.

## Key Question 4: NRC actions in 2025–2026 to speed licensing

### Takeaway
By November 2026 the NRC has (a) finalized the optional Part 53 risk-informed, technology-inclusive framework (effective April 29, 2026), (b) finalized a rule streamlining contested hearings into 18-month, 12-month and expedited tracks (Nov 3, 2026), (c) adopted generic 18-month schedules for new applications and 12 months for renewals in line with the ADVANCE Act and EO 14300, and (d) actually issued three commercial advanced-reactor/SMR construction permits in 2026 (Natrium, Clinch River BWRX-300, Long Mott Xe-100), the last two in roughly 14 months and ahead of schedule.

### Cited Findings
- ADVANCE Act (signed July 2024) directs NRC staff reviews of certain license applications to be completed within 18 months of docketing, with a two-year limit for hearings and a 25-month outer limit for final decisions — [C3 Solutions](https://c3solutions.org/policy-paper/recent-nrc-rulemakings-can-help-unlock-advanced-nuclear-energy/); [Pillsbury](https://www.pillsburylaw.com/en/news-and-insights/nrc-proposed-rule-adjudicatory-proceedings.html)
- Executive Order 14300 (May 2025) directs an 18-month deadline for a final decision on applications to construct and operate new reactors and one year for license renewals — [C3 Solutions](https://c3solutions.org/policy-paper/recent-nrc-rulemakings-can-help-unlock-advanced-nuclear-energy/)
- Executive Order 14301 (May 2025) set the goal of three DOE-authorized advanced test reactors reaching criticality by July 4, 2026 (Reactor Pilot Program) — [Energies Media](https://energiesmedia.com/nuclear-criticality-july-4-deadline-energy/)
- NRC Licensing Efficiency update: generic schedules now support 18-month reviews for all new applications, 12-month reviews for license renewals and 12 months or shorter for most amendments — [NRC ML25191A155](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2519/ML25191A155.pdf)
- Part 53 final rule: affirmed by the Commission March 25, 2026; published in the Federal Register March 30 (or April 3, per another source); effective April 29, 2026; optional alternative to Parts 50/52; technology-inclusive, risk-informed; allows a first-of-a-kind reactor to be licensed to operate before generic design certification; nine guidance documents issued; delivered more than a year ahead of NEIMA's end-2027 deadline; excludes fusion (separate rule) — [ANS](https://www.ans.org/news/2026-03-26/article-7881/nrc-unveils-part-53-final-rule/); [Hogan Lovells](https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/nrc-finalizes-new-licensing-framework-for-advanced-reactors-and-proposes-streamlined-path-for-doe); [Beveridge & Diamond](https://www.bdlaw.com/publications/nrc-finalizes-new-optional-licensing-framework-for-advanced-reactors/)
- Alongside Part 53, NRC proposed a rule allowing applicants to leverage prior DOE reactor authorizations and testing in defined circumstances — relevant to Reactor Pilot Program participants such as Oklo, Deep Fission, Aalo, Valar — [Hogan Lovells](https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/nrc-finalizes-new-licensing-framework-for-advanced-reactors-and-proposes-streamlined-path-for-doe)
- Final rule "Streamlining Contested Adjudications in Licensing Proceedings" published Nov 3, 2026 (proposed March 3, 2026): creates Standard (18-month review), Focused (12-month) and Expedited hearing tracks; 65-day initial-decision deadline (20 days longer than proposed); board deadlines extendable only in extraordinary circumstances; dropped proposed limits on non-attorney representation — [Federal Register, Nov 3, 2026](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/03/2026-22443/streamlining-contested-adjudications-in-licensing-proceedings); [Federal Register, Mar 3, 2026 proposed](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/03/03/2026-04187/streamlining-contested-adjudications-in-licensing-proceedings); [Pillsbury](https://www.pillsburylaw.com/en/news-and-insights/nrc-proposed-rule-adjudicatory-proceedings.html)
- Approvals granted in 2026: TerraPower Natrium CP (Mar 4, 2026; ~22 months from docketing, ~3 months late vs target) — [ANS](https://www.ans.org/news/article-7818/nrc-approves-terrapower-construction-permit/); TVA Clinch River BWRX-300 CP (Sept 28, 2026; ~14-month review) — [World Nuclear News](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/first-construction-permit-issued-for-bwrx300-smr-in-us); Dow/X-energy Long Mott CP (early Nov 2026, "over a month ahead of schedule") — [ANS](https://www.ans.org/news/article-8573/nrc-issues-construction-permit-for-dowxenergy-long-mott-project/)

### Inferences
- The combination of Part 53, the hearing-streamlining rule and the DOE-authorization crossover proposal gives developers three distinct routes (Part 50/52 at NRC; Part 53 at NRC; DOE authorization under the Pilot Program with a later NRC transition), and Oklo and Deep Fission have explicitly chosen the DOE route first — which shifts first-of-a-kind regulatory risk from the NRC to DOE and makes the "DOE-to-NRC transition" rule a key 2027 watch item.
- Review durations are converging on the statutory 18 months (Clinch River ~14 months, Long Mott early), supporting the case that licensing is no longer the binding constraint; financing and firm offtake are.
