## Key Question 1: Uranium prices (spot and long-term) over 2024-2026, and supply/demand fundamentals

### Takeaway
Spot U3O8 sits at roughly US$90-92/lb in mid-November 2026, below the ~US$100 January 2026 spike but well above the 2025 low of ~US$63-64, while the long-term (term) price has broken to all-time highs (TradeTech US$99.00 on 31 Oct 2026; Cameco month-end US$96.50 in Aug 2026). The structural story is a term market at record prices despite utility contracting that has run below replacement rate for a 14th consecutive year, with mine supply constrained by Kazatomprom's acid problems, slipping developer timelines and a widely cited ~212 Mlb unidentified-supply gap for 2040.

- Kazatomprom's TQZ sulfuric acid plant commissioning slipped from Q1 2027 to between Q3 2027 and Q1 2028, and the company said it will factor this into 2027 guidance; analysts estimate a Russian acid export ban without exemption could cut 2027 output by ~3 Mlb (~4%) — [Discovery Alert](https://discoveryalert.com/analysis/kazatomprom-sulfuric-acid-uranium-supply-september-2026/); [Kazatomprom 1H2026](https://www.kazatomprom.kz/en/media/view/Kazatomprom%20announces%201H2026%20Financial%20Results)

### Inferences
- The spread between term (~US$96-99) and spot (~US$90) is unusually wide and inverted relative to history; it implies utilities are paying up for security of future supply while near-term spot demand (notably from financial vehicles whose issuance is shut, see Q4) is muted.
- Equities have decoupled negatively from the commodity in 2026 (ETFs down 20-30% YTD, see Q4) despite record term prices, a divergence several sources attribute to macro/liquidity factors rather than uranium fundamentals.

### Gaps
- No single authoritative figure for the intra-month January 2026 spot high (sources range US$94-101); UxC and TradeTech daily data are paywalled.
- No UxC-sourced year-to-date term contracting total for 2026 beyond August (~37-38 Mlb).
- No official November 2026 monthly average spot price yet.
- The 46%/212 Mlb WNA supply-gap figure could not be confirmed on WNA's own site.

## Key Question 2: Major listed producers and developers — production, costs, projects, recent results

### Takeaway
Producers reported higher realized prices in Q3 2026 (Cameco, Energy Fuels US$88.90, Paladin record US$79.40, Kazatomprom US$67.88 in 1H) but mixed operations: Cameco narrowed 2026 production guidance down, Kazatomprom grew output 9% but faces acid risk, Boss is pumping harder for flat output, and US ISR producers are small but profitable at the gross-margin level. Developers are split between funded builders (NexGen, Denison, Paladin's PLS) and those deferring or struggling to finance (Deep Yellow's Tumas, Global Atomic's Dasa).
