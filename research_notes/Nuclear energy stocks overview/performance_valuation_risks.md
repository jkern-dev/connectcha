## Key Question 1: Performance across segments and ETFs (2024, 2025, 2026 YTD) and the major drawdowns/rallies

### Takeaway
Nuclear equities had a flat-to-mixed 2024 (URA roughly -0.5%; the AI-power utilities soared), a spectacular 2025 (URA +67%, NLR +56%, Oklo +238%), and a brutal 2026: as of early-to-mid November 2026 URA is down roughly 28% YTD, URNM roughly 32%, NLR roughly 23-33% depending on window, the pre-revenue SMR names are down 50-62%, and even the "quality" names (Cameco, Constellation, Vistra, Centrus) are 20-74% below their 52-week highs. The three dated shocks of 2025 (DeepSeek on 27 Jan 2025, the executive-order rally of 23 May 2025, the Westinghouse/US government term sheet rally of 28-29 Oct 2025) were followed by a sector-wide top in late October 2025 and a 2026 bear market driven by dilution, the Iran-war inflation/rate shock, the AI-trade unwind and a post-midterm regulatory scare on 4 Nov 2026.

### Inferences
- The 2026 drawdown was broad but not uniform: the SMR developers (-50% to -62% YTD) and Centrus (-74% from high) suffered most, uranium miners/ETFs fell 25-33%, Constellation roughly -20%, while GE Vernova remained positive YTD and Talen appears to have held up relatively well (its Nov 11 price of $378 sits in the upper half of its 52-week range). - The group's three 2025 "event rallies" were all policy-driven (DeepSeek was the one demand-driven shock), and 2026's selloffs were a combination of (a) company-specific dilution, (b) macro (inflation/rates), and (c) political/regulatory risk to the data-center demand thesis, which is now the single most important swing factor.

## Key Question 2: Valuation (multiples for profitable names, market cap vs cash for pre-revenue names, ETF AUM and flows)

### Takeaway
After the 2026 reset, the profitable nuclear names trade at widely dispersed multiples: Talen ~10x forward earnings, Vistra ~13-16x, Constellation ~20-24x (down from ~28x), BWXT ~28x forward (below its five-year average), and GE Vernova still ~51x forward non-GAAP P/E and ~40x trailing EV/EBITDA. Nuclear ETF assets have shrunk materially: NLR fell from $4.57B (Mar 2026) to $2.90B (13 Nov 2026).
